01 / About

A personal journey,
still being written.

This is a space for the person behind the projects: what I study, what I explore, and the questions that keep me thinking.

I’m currently a student with a strong interest in political science, history, Indian cultural traditions, and public affairs. I enjoy exploring how institutions, ideas, and civilisations evolve, and how the past can inform thoughtful approaches to the present.

This website brings together my interests and independent projects in one place. It is a work in progress, much like the person it represents: ideas may develop, positions may be refined, and new work will find its way here over time.

Beyond politics, I’m interested in religious and philosophical literature, historical interpretation, and creative alternate-history worlds.
